Debrief

What to work on

Ordered by the time on the table. Your best lap was 2:44.741. Putting your best sectors together gives 2:44.086, so 0.66 s is already in your own laps.

Les Combes

≈ 1.0 s spread

Your least consistent part of the lap. On lap 3 you braked 132 m before the apex at full pressure, dropped to 94 km/h and left at 111 km/h. On lap 4 you braked 144 m out with only 76 % pressure, carried 112 km/h through the apex and exited at 122 km/h. Lap 3 was your slowest lap, and it is where you lost the lead, dropping from P1 to P3.

Brake earlier and lighter, and trail into the first apex the way you did on lap 4. Minimum speed matters more here than braking late.

Paul Frère to Blanchimont

≈ 0.7 s spread

Lap 6 ran this part in 24.05 s. Laps 1–3 took 24.76–24.84 s. On lap 6 you held full throttle in the tow of the car ahead and hit 7,235 rpm in 5th. On lap 5 you lifted to about 72 % throttle for 600 m through Blanchimont while sitting 9 m behind the car ahead.

The GR86 can take Blanchimont flat. If you're in a tow, commit to the run and pull out before the Bus Stop braking zone instead of lifting.

Following without passing

laps 4–5

On laps 4 and 5 you held about 80 % throttle for 500 m on Kemmel, 8–13 m behind the car ahead in 5th gear. You stayed in the draft but never used it. The car behind was 300+ m away, so you had no need to defend.

Pull out of the tow by the Kemmel braking boards, or drop back 20–30 m out of Raidillon to get a better run. Lifting on the straight costs you speed and gets you no position.

Raidillon exit

2 × 1x

Both incident points came at the same place: about 1,100 m into the lap, at the top of Raidillon, at around 195 km/h. On lap 5 you used 38° of steering where you normally use 25°, with a 2.7 g lateral spike, which points to a correction. On lap 6 the steering was normal, so that one was most likely running wide on the exit and putting four wheels off.

Hold a tighter line over the crest and let the car run out to the edge, not past it. Another 2x there turns 4 incident points into 6.

Bus Stop exit

≈ 0.55 s spread

On laps 2 and 5 this section took 16.08 and 16.10 s, against 15.53 s on lap 6. On those laps your speed 150 m after the second apex was 106–107 km/h. On laps 1, 3 and 4 it was 113–119 km/h. Laps 2 and 5 each came under pressure, with a car close behind on lap 2 and just after the Raidillon moment on lap 5.

Give up a little entry speed into the second part to get the car straight and on full throttle sooner. Exit speed carries all the way to La Source.

La Source

≈ 0.8 s spread

The opening section varies from 19.85 to 20.64 s. Your best pass (lap 4) braked latest (102 m) with the lightest peak pressure (92 %) and the highest minimum speed (64 km/h). On laps 3 and 6 you braked early and harder, fell to 59 km/h and went to full throttle a little late.

Use lap 4 as your reference: brake later and less hard, and roll more speed to the apex.
